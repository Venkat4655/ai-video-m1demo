QuickCart Frequently Asked Questions

SHIPPING POLICY

Q: How long does it take to process an order?
A: Orders are processed within 1 business day.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping costs $4.99 and express shipping costs $14.99.

Q: Is there free shipping?
A: Yes. Standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more.

Q: Where do you ship?
A: We ship within the United States only.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: A tracking number is emailed once the order ships.

RETURN POLICY

Q: How long do I have to return an item?
A: Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Q: What condition must a returned item be in?
A: Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Q: Who pays for return shipping?
A: Customers pay return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Q: What items cannot be returned?
A: Gift cards, personal care items, and final sale items cannot be returned.

REFUNDS

Q: How will I get my refund?
A: Refunds go to the original payment method.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive the return.

Q: Are shipping charges refunded?
A: Original shipping charges are not refunded.

CANCELLATIONS

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: An order can be cancelled until it ships.

Q: What if my order has already shipped?
A: An order that has shipped cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS

Q: What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?
A: Report it within 7 days of delivery. We send a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping.

CONTACT

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Email support@quickcart.example. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.
